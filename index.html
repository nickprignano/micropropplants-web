Our Process

From Lab to Your Door

Every plant goes through a rigorous, multi-stage laboratory process before it's packaged and shipped to you.

1

Stock Selection

We start with thoroughly indexed, disease-free mother plants of verified genetic identity.

2

In Vitro Initiation

Explants are surface-sterilised and established on nutrient agar media inside a sterile laminar flow hood.

3

Multiplication

Shoot clumps are divided and sub-cultured through multiple cycles to build up the number of plantlets.

4

Rooting & Dispatch

Rooted plantlets are hardened off, quality-checked, and carefully packaged for safe nationwide delivery.
